Privacy Notice (Summary)

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This is an English summary for convenience. The legally binding version is the Spanish privacy notice, issued under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties.

01Who is responsible for your data

4Ward Solutions, S.A. de C.V. ("4ward"), Río San Joaquín 38, Col. Anáhuac, Mexico City. Privacy contact: rvertti@4wardcorp.com.

02What we collect

Only what you give us through the contact form or by email: your name, company, email, phone (optional), industry (optional) and a description of the process you want to improve. We do not collect sensitive or financial data; please don't include any in your message.

03Why we use it

Only to respond to your request, hold conversations about your needs, prepare and send a proposal if you ask for one, and follow up on it and on any resulting business relationship. No marketing, no newsletters.

04Who we share it with

We don't sell or transfer your data. Our web hosting and email provider, Hostinger, processes it only on our behalf. We would disclose it to an authority only if required by law or a lawful order.

05How long we keep it

While we handle your request and, if no business relationship results, up to 24 months after our last contact; then we delete it securely. If we sign a contract, we keep it for the contract's term and as long as the law requires.

06How we protect it

Encrypted connection (HTTPS), access limited to the people handling your request, and password-protected accounts.

07Your rights

You can limit the use of your data, withdraw your consent, or exercise your ARCO rights — access, rectification, cancellation and objection — by emailing rvertti@4wardcorp.com with the subject "Derechos ARCO". The Spanish notice lists what the request must include. We reply within 20 business days and act within the following 15 (each can be extended once). It's free of charge, except for any shipping or copying costs.

08Cookies

This site uses no tracking or advertising cookies, no analytics, and loads no third-party files. It only stores in your browser the language you chose (Spanish or English), so it can show you that version on your next visits; this is never sent to 4ward or anyone else.

09Changes

Any change will be posted on the Spanish notice with its update date. If we ever need your consent for a new purpose, we'll ask first.

10Authority

If you believe your data protection rights were violated, you may contact Mexico's Ministry of Anticorruption and Good Governance (Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno).
